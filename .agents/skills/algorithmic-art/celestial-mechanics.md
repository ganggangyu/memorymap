# Celestial Mechanics

## An Antique Orrery of Memory

**The Algorithmic Foundation** builds a heliocentric model through Kepler-inspired orbital mechanics. Each memory-point becomes a planet with a unique elliptical orbit, its semi-major axis and eccentricity seeded by the memory's creation date and importance. The central sun is not a luminous disc but a warm, breathing glow — achieved through layered concentric circles whose opacity oscillates at prime-number frequencies, creating a flicker that evokes candlelight rather than fusion fire. This is a meticulously crafted system where every orbital parameter was calibrated through iterative refinement to produce natural, unhurried motion that rewards patient observation.

**Orbital Architecture** follows a hierarchy: the most significant memories (milestones, anniversaries) occupy inner orbits with faster periods and brighter glows, while everyday memories drift in slower, wider orbits. Each planet's size maps to the number of photos attached to that memory — a subtle encoding that reveals meaning only to those who look closely. The orbital paths themselves are drawn as delicate gold ink ellipses on parchment, their eccentricity varying slightly across bodies to avoid the sterile uniformity of perfect circles. Between these threads of gold, faint perturbation lines ripple outward — the gravitational whisper of one memory influencing another, a computational echo of love's interconnectedness.

**The Color System** draws from Renaissance astronomical charts: warm parchment cream for the cosmic background, deep indigo ink for the orbital paths, gold leaf for active constellation threads, and cinnabar seal-red for milestone markers. Each planet receives a unique hue sampled from a carefully curated palette of vintage pigments — Venetian red, Prussian blue, yellow ochre, viridian — ensuring visual distinction while maintaining the hand-tinted aesthetic of antique star atlases. The central sun glows in layered gold-to-amber, its corona rendered as soft radial gradients that pulse with the steady rhythm of a well-made clockwork.

**Planetary Bodies** are not simple dots but textured spheres generated through layered Perlin noise mapped to spherical coordinates. Each planet carries subtle surface features — darker maria, lighter highlands — that rotate independently of the orbital motion, creating the illusion of worlds turning in space. The algorithm renders each body through a meticulously optimized painter's algorithm: base color, shadow hemisphere, specular highlight, and atmospheric rim glow, composed in that order to simulate volumetric form on a flat canvas. The largest memories receive faint ring systems — a computational nod to Saturn — rendered as translucent ellipses whose particle density follows a Gaussian distribution.

**Temporal Evolution** unfolds through three nested time systems: the fast rotation of individual planets (8-15 second periods), the mid-speed orbital revolution (45-180 second periods depending on orbit radius), and a slow pan-and-zoom of the entire orrery that occasionally brings distant memories into focus. A master-level generative artist calibrated these three clocks to avoid any perceptible looping within a 10-minute observation window. The system includes occasional "conjunctions" — two memory-planets aligning in the same angular sector — that trigger a brief brightening of both bodies and their connecting thread, making the cosmic dance feel alive and responsive rather than mechanical.
